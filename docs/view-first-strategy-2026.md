# Workflow Guide — View-First Strategy 2026

## Why the pivot
Current 2026 platform signals favour curiosity, useful discovery, original creator-led content and stronger retention over generic mass-produced short-form. TikTok's own 2026 trend report emphasises curiosity/search, useful discovery and human relevance. Metricool's 2026 data shows TikTok still leads short-video reach while Instagram Reels lead interactions; TikTok is also more saturated, so quality and distinctiveness matter.

## Chosen niche
Start narrower than "all technology":
**Digital everyday myths for smartphone-first people.**

Examples:
- what Incognito actually does
- why phones slow charging around 80%
- passkeys vs passwords
- QR phishing
- why full Wi-Fi bars can still mean slow internet
- what deleting a file really means
- why browsers use so much RAM
- what a VPN changes and what it doesn't

This niche is broad enough for hundreds of evergreen topics, but coherent enough for viewers and algorithms to understand the account.

## Format
Short-form:
- 65–90 seconds
- original German narration
- Mika as recurring fictional host
- diagrams and animated visual explanations
- search-friendly title/hook
- no product selling
- no outbound CTA
- one comment question

Long-form:
- 8–12 minute YouTube explainers
- built from topic clusters, not stitched Shorts
- new examples, deeper explanations and transitions
- purpose: watch time and eventual YPP revenue

## Monetisation ladder
1. Audience + qualified watch time
2. TikTok Creator Rewards where eligible
3. YouTube Partner Program / watch-page and Shorts monetisation when thresholds are met
4. Optional later sponsorships / affiliate only where genuinely relevant
5. Existing affiliate site remains available but is no longer the editorial core

## AI transparency
Mika is fictional and AI-generated.
- TikTok: realistic AI-generated visual/audio must be labelled according to platform AI-content rules.
- YouTube: realistic synthetic content must be disclosed in the upload flow.
- Instagram/Meta: retain metadata/disclosures and do not imply Mika is a real person.
