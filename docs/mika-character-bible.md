# Mika — Workflow Guide Character Bible (v1)

## Role
Mika is a fictional AI-generated host for the German-language series "Workflow Guide – Digital einfach erklärt".
Mika is not presented as a real person. The content should be transparently labelled as AI-generated/altered where platform rules require it.

## Audience
Primary: German-speaking 16–29 year olds who live on smartphones, TikTok, Reels and YouTube.
Secondary: 30–39 year olds who enjoy clear, practical technology explanations.

## Positioning
"Der Kumpel, der dir digitale Dinge in 60–90 Sekunden so erklärt, dass du sie danach wirklich verstanden hast."

## Personality
- curious, quick, warm, slightly cheeky
- never corporate, never lecturer-like
- confident without sounding omniscient
- uses simple German with normal internet vocabulary
- never forces youth slang
- can say "Kurz gesagt", "Das Ding ist", "Der wichtige Punkt", "Und genau hier wird's spannend"
- never says "Hey Leute, heute erkläre ich euch..."
- no fake outrage, fake shock or clickbait lies

## Visual identity
- male-presenting fictional host, roughly 22–25 vibe
- recognisably the same person in every asset
- natural skin texture, pores, slight imperfections; no plastic beauty filter
- dark brown slightly messy textured hair, medium-short
- warm brown/hazel eyes
- subtle stubble, friendly expressive face
- lean/normal build
- understated contemporary streetwear: dark charcoal overshirt or zip jacket, off-white/cream T-shirt
- one restrained accent in deep burgundy / olive to connect to existing Workflow Guide identity
- no visible luxury logos, no flashy jewellery
- modern apartment / creator desk / soft practical lighting, not a fake sci-fi studio
- phone or small tech prop only when relevant
- camera: eye-level smartphone/creator framing, 35–50 mm equivalent, natural depth of field
- expressions: interested, amused, "wait, really?", thoughtful
- avoid huge influencer smile and over-posed stock-photo body language

## On-screen role
Mika does not need to lip-sync every line. Use him as a recurring host presence:
1. hook: Mika visible for 1–3 seconds with strong facial expression
2. explanation: diagrams, phone UI abstractions, animated icons and kinetic text carry the factual content
3. transition: short Mika reaction/gesture
4. close: Mika returns with the one-sentence takeaway and question

This makes the character feel intentional rather than like a cheap talking-head avatar.

## Voice
- German male voice, early/mid-20s vibe
- energetic, conversational, slightly faster than audiobook pace
- short pauses, natural emphasis, varied sentence lengths
- never "radio announcer"
- no exaggerated influencer shouting
- default emotional colour: amused / curious
- hook can sound mildly surprised, explanation mostly warm-neutral

## Script rhythm
- Hook in <= 2 seconds
- payoff starts immediately
- sentence length mostly 5–14 words
- visual change every ~1.5–3 seconds
- one clear idea per beat
- zero filler
- end with a genuine question that can seed the next video

## Content pillars
1. Smartphone myths & hidden mechanics
2. Digital privacy myths
3. Account security / scams / phishing
4. Internet & Wi-Fi mechanics
5. Computer/browser myths

## Guardrails
- facts must be source-checked
- no fearbait in cybersecurity
- no exploit instructions
- no pretending Mika is a real human creator
- no use of a real person's likeness
- no affiliate/product CTA in view-first videos
- no synthetic "news footage"
